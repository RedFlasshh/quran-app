Privacy Policy

Understanding the Quran

Last updated: August 29, 2026

This policy explains what information the Understanding the Quran app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We built this app to help people study the Quran chapter by chapter, and we collect the minimum information needed to make that work.

Information we collect

You can use the app and read every chapter without creating an account. If you choose to sign in (using Google or email), we collect:

  • Account information — your email address, used to identify your account.
  • App activity — which chapters you've read and your quiz results, so your progress can be saved and synced across your devices.
We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, financial information, or health information. We do not show ads, and we do not use any analytics or advertising SDKs.

How we use your information

Your email and progress data are used solely to operate your account and to save your reading progress. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Where your data is stored

Account and progress data are stored securely with Supabase, our backend provider, using encrypted connections (HTTPS). If you never sign in, no personal data leaves your device.

Data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us at ygm786@gmail.com. We will process deletion requests within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

Understanding the Quran is not directed at children and is not designed to appeal primarily to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to ygm786@gmail.com.
